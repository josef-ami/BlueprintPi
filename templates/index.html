Lidar pipeline  ·  front —  ·  —

1 · Raw

Every return, coloured by signal quality (red weak → green strong).

2 · Filtered

Survivors of the quality + isolation filters. Compare against Raw to see what was dropped.

3 · Obstacles

Clusters, coloured by camera. Grey ring = UNKNOWN. Thin rays = camera colour bearings.

4 · Nearest-neighbour distance

Each quality-passed point's distance to its closest neighbour. Set the neighbour distance line to the right of the cluster peak, left of the noise.

5 · Cluster gap

Gaps between angularly-adjacent survivors. Set the cluster gap line in the valley between within-cluster gaps and between-cluster gaps.
